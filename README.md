# House Redstone of The Iron Coast

## Timestamps

### 209 AC: The Shadows of Ashford

After his father's recent death, young Robar is Lord of the Iron Coast. His brother, the regent Osmund governs the lands with an iron fist, who, in his blood-crazed vendetta against the Mountain Clans, massacres innocent farmers and puts villages to the torch. Robar's aunt Alayne, returned from King's Landing after her husband's death and took her nephew under her wings. The Red Widow and Mad Osmund are competing to influence the young lord.

### 258 AC: War of the Ninepenny Kings

First years of Lord Elbert, making use of the Redstone fleet. Few years before the Reyne-Tarbeck revolt.

### 282 AC: Robert's Rebellion

Siege of Gulltown.

### 284 AC: The Crowned Stag

Final years of Lord Elbert.
